## 🌌 Project Intelligence

DaTeam has engineered a high-performance, **device-agnostic centralized management solution** designed to modernize vehicle rental services. Our system targets a massive ecosystem of **30,000 university students and staff** within the UTM community. By deploying a synchronized digital lifecycle, we have eliminated the friction of manual "AS-IS" workflows.

---

## 🛠️ System Architecture Evolution

### 🔴 Phase 1: Problem Extraction & Proposal
* **Identified Friction**: Heavy reliance on WhatsApp led to slow manual confirmations and booking overlaps.
* **Mission Objectives**: Centralize fragmented business activities into four primary modules: **User, Booking, Fleet, and Reporting**.
* **Strategic Scope**: Focused exclusively on vehicle rental operations to ensure maximum reliability and system focus.

### 🟡 Phase 2: Conceptual & Transactional Design
* **Business Rules**: Engineered automated deposit handling and mileage-based maintenance alerts.
* **Compliance Engineering**: Integrated audit trails for summons tracking and insurance verification.
* **Security Architecture**: Implemented **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)** to protect privacy while empowering staff and admins with relevant data.

### 🟢 Phase 3: Logical Power & Implementation
* **Normalization**: Refined data structures to **Third Normal Form (3NF)** to ensure zero redundancy and high data integrity.
* **Performance Analytics**: Developed a real-time dashboard for fleet monitoring and financial KPI visualization.
* **Retention Engine**: Launched a **Loyalty & Rewards** module to drive customer engagement through point-based incentives.
